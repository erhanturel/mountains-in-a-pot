Mountains in a Pot · the core on two pages · v0.1

Shape the ground. The world grows. Count what bloomed.

The only builder where you place nothing — you shape the ground, and a living world grows itself.

The core loop (one Year)

1 · Shape12 Moons. Each Moon: act (Uplift, Subduct, rain, seed — costs mana) or rest (free). Either way the world runs 12 ticks: water walks, soil moves, life spreads.
2 · HarvestCount what stands. Bloom = living hexes × (1 + Wonders). Wonders are shapes the water made: a Lake, a River, a Waterfall, an Island, a Ring of Life.
3 · The barBloom ≥ this Year's target → continue. Below it → the pot rests: the run ends on the Behold screen. That is the only fail.
4 · SpringSurplus Bloom becomes Dew. Shop: 3 Boons (permanent rule changes) + 2 Seed cards (kinds of life). Same pot, next Year, higher bar.

Eight Years in one pot. Year 1 is a puddle and a patch of moss; Year 8 is a world — or the pot rests earlier, and you behold what you grew.

Moment to moment

  • Read the pot: where would water go, where is soil gathering, where can life reach.
  • Spend a Moon: dig a basin (1 mana), raise a ridge (2), hang a cloud (3), sow a seed (1), or rest (0).
  • Watch 12 ticks: the basin fills, the shore greens one hex a tick, the slag fan grows.
  • Plan the next Moon from what you saw. Nothing moves while you think; nothing is random.

What you win by

  • Count: living hexes. Life needs a course of soil, not drowned, and visible water beside it (a lake tile or a river).
  • Shape: Wonders multiply the count. They are about water and terrain, so "cover everything in moss" never wins.
  • Build: Boons + seeds + the shape you carved agreeing — Lake, River, Highland, Sprawl.
